Project Infinity-X 4.2 for Xiaomi Redmi 13 5G / POCO M6 Plus 5G (breeze)
Release Date: 09.11.2026
========================================================================

Camera & Imaging:
- Fix inverted/bluish colors and orange tint across Photo, Portrait, and Night modes (restored proper UV chroma plane alignment in libcamera_mianode_jni and libcamera_algoup_jni)
- Fix photo saving pipeline: restored standard isParallel queue handling in MiuiCamera to ensure captured images save reliably to storage
- Restore continuous 3A convergence (AWB/AE) via camera.disable_zsl_mode=0 for true-to-life color rendering
- Full support for 108MP Ultra HD mode with hardware remosaic and downscale bypass
- Include Google Photos prebuilt while preserving Glimpse Gallery

Radio & Telephony:
- Revert experimental radio RAT combining and forced dual connectivity properties
- Fix SIM detected but "No Service" on boot
- Sync modem procedure byte handling (procedure_bytes=SKIP) and APM power states with stock HyperOS

Display & Power:
- Correct default peak refresh rate to native 120Hz panel ceiling
- Enable dynamic refresh rate downscaling with 200ms idle timer
- Enable asynchronous display power mode for instant screen off
- Disable Always-On Display (AOD) by default for IPS LCD backlight power saving
- Restore pickup-to-wake gesture pulse

System & Hardware:
- Full room-wide Consumer IR blaster range and zero transmission latency via SPI carrier modulation fix
- Add Novatek and Focaltech touchscreen recovery firmware and touch calibration
- Integrate official InfinityX-OTA updater endpoints
