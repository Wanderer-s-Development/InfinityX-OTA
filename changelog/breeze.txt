Project Infinity-X 4.2 for Xiaomi Redmi 13 5G / POCO M6 Plus 5G (breeze)
Release Date: 09.11.2026
========================================================================

Camera & Imaging:
- Fix Inverted / Blue Color Defect: Corrected native NV21 chroma plane base offset in libcamera_mianode_jni.xiaomi.so and libcamera_algoup_jni.xiaomi.so (mov w2, #2), eliminating inverted colors and cyan skin tones across all captured photos
- Fix HDR Controls: Restored HDR toggle button (Auto / On / Off) in HyperOS Camera top bar by bypassing AOSP CamX vendor tag check

- Fix photo saving pipeline: restored standard isParallel queue handling in MiuiCamera to ensure captured images save reliably to storage
- Restore continuous 3A convergence (AWB/AE) via camera.disable_zsl_mode=0 for true-to-life color rendering
- Full support for 108MP Ultra HD mode with hardware remosaic and downscale bypass
- Include Google Photos prebuilt while preserving Glimpse Gallery

- Enable dynamic refresh rate downscaling with 200ms idle timer
- Enable asynchronous display power mode for instant screen off
